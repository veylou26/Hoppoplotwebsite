About us

Making your own neighborhood feel new

HippoPlot is a local discovery app that helps people find what's happening near them and helps independent businesses reach the community around them.

Our mission

The best things to do are often a few blocks away: a pop-up market, a new café, a gallery opening, a class at the studio down the street. They're easy to miss because they're scattered across dozens of websites, flyers and social accounts.

HippoPlot brings them together in one place, organized around where you are. Our mission is to help people spend more time in their own communities and to give independent businesses a fair way to be found by their neighbors.

What we build

  • Feed: posts, deals and live updates from local businesses and people.
  • Map: businesses and upcoming events nearby, with category and date filters.
  • AI Gems: events our AI collects from public listings, each linked to its original source.
  • Plans: a simple way to invite friends, suggest options and vote on what to do.
  • HippoPlot Business: one all-inclusive plan for $29/month with unlimited posts and event listings, deals, a custom Action Button, Feed priority placement, Quick Replies and analytics.

How we work

Local first. We show what's near you, not what's trending on the other side of the world.

Honest numbers. Business analytics come from real activity in the app: views, clicks, saves and follows.

Safety and privacy. Content is moderated, people can report and block, location sharing is optional, and you can delete your account at any time.
