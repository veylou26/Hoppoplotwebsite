Account deletion

Delete your HippoPlot account

How to permanently delete your HippoPlot account and the data associated with it. HippoPlot is developed and operated by MOHA TECHNOLOGIES LLC.

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the HippoPlot app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Security.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap delete.

Your account is deleted right away. If you have an active HippoPlot Business subscription, it's cancelled automatically as part of the deletion, so you won't be billed again.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't sign in to the app, email support@hippoplot.com from the email address on your HippoPlot account, with the subject "Account deletion request". Include your username if you know it.

We'll verify that the request comes from the account owner, delete the account, and confirm by email, normally within 7 days and always within the time required by applicable law.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details, profile, username, photos and settings
  • Business profile information (business name, address, hours, contact details, menu)
  • Your posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, saves, collections and live updates
  • Messages you sent, and conversations you started
  • Plans you created, and your votes and memberships in other Plans
  • Follows, friend connections and your blocked-users list
  • Saved places and events, notifications, stored location and feedback you sent
  • Events and deals your business listed, and Brand Circle memberships
  • Your HippoPlot Business subscription record (the subscription is cancelled in Stripe first)

What we keep, and for how long

  • Billing records: invoices and payment records for HippoPlot Business are kept by our payment processor, Stripe, for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Moderation reports: reports you filed about content are kept for safety history, with your identity removed.
  • Legal holds: information we must keep to comply with the law, resolve disputes or prevent fraud and abuse, for only as long as required.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period until they're overwritten on our provider's regular backup schedule.

Copies of content that other people have already shared outside HippoPlot can't be recalled.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual posts, comments and saves in the app at any time. To ask us to delete other specific data, such as your stored location, email support@hippoplot.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
