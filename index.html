What you can do

Four ways to find what's near you

One app for the cafés, shops, studios, markets and events within reach, without scrolling through content from the other side of the world.

Feed

Photos, videos, deals and live updates from businesses and people near you. Like, save, comment and share the posts you care about.

Map

See local businesses and upcoming events on one map. Filter by category or by date, such as today, this weekend or a day you pick.

AI Gems

Events our AI finds in public listings for your area, each with a link back to the original source so you can check the details.

Plans

Start a plan, invite friends, add events or your own ideas, and vote together on what to do.

How it works

From "what's on?" to "see you there"

Choose your area

Turn on location to see what's nearby, or choose a city yourself. Location is optional, and you can change it at any time.

Browse and save

Scroll the Feed, explore the Map and open AI Gems. Save places and events to collections you can come back to.

Go together

Share a find in a message or add it to a Plan, then decide with friends where to go.

Safety & privacy

Built for real neighborhoods

Moderated content

Photos, videos and captions are checked automatically before they appear publicly, and our team reviews reports.

Location is your choice

HippoPlot uses your location only with your permission, to show what's nearby. You can browse by choosing a city instead.

Report and block

Report posts, events or people from inside the app, and block anyone you don't want to hear from.

Your data, your call

Delete your account from Settings at any time, or contact us to access or remove your data. See our Privacy Policy.
