This Privacy Policy explains how MOHA TECHNOLOGIES LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the HippoPlot mobile apps, the HippoPlot web app and the hippoplot.com website (together, the "Services").

1. Who we are

HippoPlot is operated by MOHA TECHNOLOGIES LLC, headquartered in Clifton, New Jersey, USA. MOHA TECHNOLOGIES LLC is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at support@hippoplot.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address and password (passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), or your basic Google account details (name, email address and profile photo) if you sign in with Google. We also collect the account type you choose (personal or business).
  • Profile information: your display name, username, profile and cover photos, bio and preferences. Business accounts may also add a business name, description, category, address, hours, phone number, website, service menu and logo, which are shown publicly.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, videos, captions, comments, likes, saves, collections, event listings, deals, live updates, Plans and the options and votes in them, and Quick Replies.
  • Messages: direct and group messages you send through HippoPlot. Messages are stored on our servers so they can be delivered and shown to you. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Reports, feedback and support: content you report, ratings and comments you send through in-app feedback (together with your app version and account type), and messages you send to our support team.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: if you allow it, your device's location, used to show nearby places and events (see Location).
  • Usage information: how you use the Services, such as the screens and features you open and the posts you view or interact with. When you view or tap a business's post, we record that interaction so the business can see aggregate counts of post views, clicks, shares and profile taps.
  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, app version, language, IP address, and diagnostic and log data.
  • Local storage: the web app and website use browser storage and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and measure product usage. The hippoplot.com website does not use advertising cookies.

Payment information

HippoPlot Business subscriptions are purchased on the web through Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your card details. We do not receive or store full card numbers. We do receive information such as your Stripe customer ID, subscription status, plan and billing period.

Information from public sources

AI Gems are events we collect from publicly available event listings and websites. They may include the names of venues or organizers that appear in those public listings.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Services: create and manage your account, show your feed, map and AI Gems, deliver messages and Plans, and show your public profile and posts.
  • To personalize what you see, such as nearby businesses and events, based on the area you choose or your location.
  • To run HippoPlot Business: process subscriptions, enable paid features, and give business accounts analytics about their own posts.
  • To keep HippoPlot safe: automatically screen photos, videos and captions for prohibited content, review reports, prevent spam, fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To communicate with you: send service messages, account and billing notices, and in-app notifications you've turned on, and answer support requests.
  • To understand and improve the Services: measure feature usage, fix bugs and develop new features.
  • To comply with law, and to protect the rights, property and safety of our users, the public and MOHA TECHNOLOGIES LLC.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your personal information for targeted advertising by third parties. We do not use your posts or messages to create AI Gems.

4. How information is shared

  • With other users: your public profile, posts, comments, business details and live updates can be seen by other HippoPlot users. People you message see the messages you send them. Members of a Plan see its options, votes and members. Businesses see aggregate counts of activity on their posts, and may see the usernames of people who follow them or like their posts.
  • With service providers who process information for us under contract, as described below.
  • For legal reasons: when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is needed to protect someone's safety or to investigate fraud or abuse.
  • In a business transfer: if MOHA TECHNOLOGIES LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction, for example when you share a post to another app.

6. Location

With your permission, HippoPlot uses your device's precise location while you use the app to center the Map and show nearby posts, businesses and events. Location is optional. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in your device settings and choose a city instead. We do not share your precise location with other users. Business addresses are public and are shown on the Map.

7. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, content, messages you sent, saves, Plans, settings and uploaded files, as described below. We may keep limited information for longer when necessary to comply with legal, tax or accounting obligations (for example, billing records kept by Stripe), to resolve disputes, or to enforce our terms. Reports you filed are kept for moderation history with your identity removed. Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most of your information in Settings. For anything else, email support@hippoplot.com.
  • Copy of your data: you can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account in the app at any time (see below).
  • Location and notifications: manage these in your device settings and in HippoPlot's Notification Preferences.
  • Visibility and blocking: manage privacy options and blocked people in Settings → Privacy & Visibility.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or other U.S. states, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights, such as the right to know, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise them, email support@hippoplot.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we respond within the time required by applicable law. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Deleting your account

In the app: go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete My Account and confirm. This cancels any active HippoPlot Business subscription and permanently deletes your account and associated data.

By email: if you can't access the app, email support@hippoplot.com from the address on your account with the subject "Account deletion request". We'll verify your request and confirm when it's done.

Step-by-step instructions, and a list of what is deleted and what is kept, are on our account deletion page.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and database security rules that limit who can read or change data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your login details private.

11. Children

HippoPlot is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in your country) to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International users

HippoPlot is operated from the United States. If you use the Services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored and processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect, and we will update the "Last updated" date above.
