Use the menu on any post, event or profile to report it. For urgent safety concerns, email us with "Safety" in the subject line.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open HippoPlot and tap Get Started. You can sign up with your email address or with Google. During setup, choose a personal account to discover places and events, or a business account if you represent a venue, shop or service.

If you didn't get a verification email, check your spam folder, then contact us if it still hasn't arrived.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Location helps HippoPlot show what's close to you on the Map and in the Nearby feed, but it's optional. You can deny the permission and choose a city yourself, and you can change the permission at any time in your device settings.

What is HippoPlot Business and how much does it cost?

HippoPlot Business is our single, all-inclusive plan for business accounts. It costs $29 per month (USD, plus any applicable taxes). New subscribers pay $1 for the first month, then $29/month. A payment card is required.

It includes unlimited posts and event listings, Deals, an Action Button with your own custom label, priority placement in the Feed, Brand Circle, Live Updates, Quick Replies, and the Business Hub with analytics for post views, clicks, saves and followers.

Subscriptions are purchased on the web through Stripe from your HippoPlot business account. Free business accounts can still create a profile and publish a limited number of posts each month.

How do I cancel or change my business subscription?

Sign in to your business account and go to Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. This opens the secure Stripe billing portal, where you can update your card, download invoices or cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep access until then.

For billing questions, email support@hippoplot.com from your account's email address.

How do I report a post, event, message or person?

Tap the menu (…) on a post, event, profile or conversation and choose Report. Our team reviews reports and may remove content or restrict accounts that break our Terms of Service. You can also block someone so they can't message you or see your posts. Manage blocked people in Settings → Privacy & Visibility.

If an AI Gem shows the wrong date, place or details, report it from the event and we'll correct or remove it.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete My Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and associated content, such as your profile, posts, comments, saves, plans and messages, as described in our Privacy Policy. Deleting your account also cancels any active HippoPlot Business subscription, so you won't be billed again.

If you can't access the app, email support@hippoplot.com with the subject "Account deletion request" from the email address on your account. We'll verify the request and confirm when it's done.

Can I get a copy of my data or correct it?

You can edit most profile information directly in Settings. To request a copy of your personal data, or to correct anything you can't edit yourself, email support@hippoplot.com with the subject "Data request". We'll respond within the time required by applicable law.

Where do AI Gems come from?

AI Gems are events that our AI collects from publicly available event listings in your area. Each AI Gem links back to its original source. Details can change, so check with the organizer before you go.
